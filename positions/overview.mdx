## Positions Page

The Positions page shows all your open and historical positions with real-time P&L and risk metrics.

## Open Positions

Each open position displays:

### Position Details

- **Market** — The perpetual contract (e.g., BTC/USD)
- **Direction** — Long or Short
- **Size** — Number of contracts or notional value
- **Leverage** — 1x to 100x
- **Entry Price** — Your average entry price
- **Current Price** — Latest market price

### Risk Metrics

- **Liquidation Price** — Price at which position closes
- **Margin Used** — Collateral allocated to this position
- **P&L** — Unrealized profit or loss
- **P&L %** — Percentage return on this position

### Actions

For each position, you can:

- **Close** — Exit the entire position immediately
- **Close Partial** — Exit part of the position
- **Set Take-Profit** — Auto-close at profit target
- **Set Stop-Loss** — Auto-close to limit losses

## Understanding Liquidation

### Liquidation Price

Your liquidation price is calculated as:

```
For Long Positions:
Liquidation = Entry Price × (1 - 1 / Leverage × (1 - Maintenance %))

For Short Positions:
Liquidation = Entry Price × (1 + 1 / Leverage × (1 - Maintenance %))
```

**Example (Long 1 BTC at $50,000 with 5x leverage):**
- Margin: $10,000
- Maintenance margin: 5%
- Liquidation price: ~$45,000

If price drops to $45,000, your position auto-closes.

### Avoiding Liquidation

1. **Monitor liquidation distance** — If it's close, reduce position
2. **Keep margin ratio high** — Stay above 200% for comfort
3. **Use stops** — Close at a small loss rather than face liquidation
4. **Don't over-leverage** — 1-5x leverage is safer than 50x+
5. **Size appropriately** — Only use capital you can afford to lose

## Closing Positions

### Market Close

Immediately close at current market price:

1. Click **Close** on the position
2. Review execution price
3. Confirm the transaction
4. Position closes, P&L realized

### Partial Close

Close part of a position:

1. Click **Close Partial**
2. Enter the amount to close
3. Confirm the transaction
4. Remaining position stays open

### Setting Exit Targets

#### Take-Profit

Auto-close at your profit target:

1. Click **Set Take-Profit**
2. Enter your target price
3. Confirm
4. When price reaches target, position closes at market

#### Stop-Loss

Auto-close to limit losses:

1. Click **Set Stop-Loss**
2. Enter your stop price
3. Confirm
4. When price reaches stop, position closes at market

## Position History

Below your open positions, see your closed positions:

- **Market** — The contract traded
- **Entry/Exit** — Entry and exit prices
- **Size** — Position notional value
- **Realized P&L** — Profit or loss realized
- **Duration** — How long position was held

## Fees and Costs

Your position incurs several fees:

### Opening Fee
Paid when you enter. Usually 0.1% of notional value.

### Closing Fee
Paid when you exit. Usually 0.1% of notional value.

### Holding Fee
Paid continuously for keeping position open. Based on:
- Position size
- Duration
- Market funding rates

### Liquidation Fee
If liquidated, you pay a fee (usually 5% of position size) to liquidators.

<Tip>
  Minimize liquidation risk by closing positions before reaching danger zone.
</Tip>

## Position P&L Tracking

### Unrealized P&L

Your profit or loss if you closed right now:

```
Unrealized P&L = (Current Price - Entry Price) × Size × Direction
```

**Long example:**
- Entry: $50,000
- Current: $51,000
- Size: 1 BTC
- P&L: +$1,000

**Short example:**
- Entry: $50,000
- Current: $49,000
- Size: 1 BTC
- P&L: +$1,000

### Realized P&L

Your actual profit or loss after closing and paying all fees.

```
Realized P&L = Exit Price - Entry Price - Opening Fee - Closing Fee - Holding Fees
```

## Position NFTs

Each position is represented as an ERC-721 NFT. You can:

- **View on blockchain** — See position details on-chain

- **Sell** — Trade positions on NFT marketplaces

Your position NFT is your proof of ownership and claim to P&L.

## Best Practices

1. **Monitor daily** — Check positions every day
2. **Set stops early** — Don't wait for bad news
3. **Take profits** — Don't be greedy, lock wins
4. **Size correctly** — Risk 1-2% per position maximum
5. **Diversify** — Spread capital across markets
6. **Track P&L** — Understand your performance over time

## Advanced Position Management

### Scaling In
Add to winning positions gradually with limit orders at higher prices.

### Scaling Out
Close positions in stages to lock in profits while keeping some exposure.

### Hedging
Open opposite positions to protect against adverse moves.

### Rolling
Close old positions and open new ones at better prices.

## Troubleshooting

**Position shows as open but I want to close?**
- Ensure you have sufficient collateral for transaction
- Check your network connection
- Try closing via manual withdraw if needed

**Liquidation price changed?**
- Funding rates or fees may have changed
- Your margin ratio may have shifted
- Refresh to see updated calculations

**Can't close partial?**
- Minimum position size may apply
- You may need to close the entire position
