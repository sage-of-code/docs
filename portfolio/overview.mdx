## Portfolio Page

The Portfolio page is your account command center. Here you can:

- Monitor your collateral balance
- Track your equity and margin utilization
- View deposit and withdrawal history
- Manage your account settings

## Account Balance

### Collateral Balance

Your collateral is your trading capital. It's:

- **Held in the protocol** in secure smart contracts
- **Yours to control** — Only you can withdraw it
- **Used as margin** — Supports your leveraged positions
- **Used for fees** — Pays trading and holding fees

### Total Equity

Total Equity = Collateral + Position P&L

This is your actual account value including open position profits and losses.

### Free Collateral

Free Collateral = Total Equity - Used Margin

This is the amount available to open new positions. When this reaches zero, you're fully deployed.

## Margin Management

### Margin Ratio

Your margin ratio shows your safety distance from liquidation:

```
Margin Ratio = Free Collateral / Used Margin × 100%
```

**Interpretation:**
- **Above 200%** — Safe, comfortable buffer
- **100-200%** — Caution zone, monitor positions
- **Below 100%** — Danger zone, liquidation risk
- **0%** — Liquidation imminent

The yellow line at 100% indicates liquidation level. Cross it and your positions auto-close.

### Position P&L

Your profit or loss on each open position is shown in real time:

- **Unrealized P&L** — Profit/loss if you closed now
- **Realized P&L** — Profit/loss on closed positions

### Liquidation Price

Each position has a liquidation price. If your margin ratio drops to 0%, liquidation occurs at this price.

## Deposits and Withdrawals

### Depositing Collateral

To deposit:

1. Click **Deposit**
2. Select the asset (USDC, USDT, etc.)
3. Enter the amount
4. Approve the token transfer (first time only)
5. Confirm the deposit
6. Wait for blockchain confirmation

Your collateral appears in your account immediately after confirmation.

### Withdrawing Collateral

To withdraw:

1. Click **Withdraw**
2. Select the asset
3. Enter the amount (must be ≤ free collateral)
4. Confirm the withdrawal
5. Wait for blockchain confirmation

Withdrawals typically take 1-2 minutes to appear in your wallet.

<Warning>
  You cannot withdraw collateral that's being used as margin for open positions. Close positions first.
</Warning>

## History

The Portfolio page shows your transaction history:

- **Date** — When the transaction occurred
- **Type** — Deposit or withdrawal
- **Amount** — Asset and quantity
- **Status** — Confirmed, pending, or failed
- **Tx Hash** — Link to blockchain explorer

Click any transaction to see full details.

## Account Settings

From the Portfolio page, you can:

- View your wallet address
- Change display preferences
- Enable/disable features
- Set notification preferences

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Equity** | Your actual account value |
| **Free Collateral** | Available to open new positions |
| **Used Margin** | Currently locked in open positions |
| **Margin Ratio** | Distance from liquidation (100% = at risk) |
| **Unrealized P&L** | Profit/loss if you closed all now |
| **Realized P&L** | Profit/loss on closed positions |

## Tips for Portfolio Management

1. **Keep margin ratio above 150%** — Gives you a safety buffer
2. **Monitor free collateral** — Don't get overextended
3. **Close losing positions early** — Don't wait for recovery
4. **Take profits regularly** — Lock in gains, withdraw excess
5. **Track your P&L** — Understand where profits come from

## Staying Safe

- **Never margin call** — Keep buffer above 100%
- **Use stops** — Automate risk management
- **Size positions appropriately** — Start small, scale up
- **Diversify** — Don't put all capital in one market
- **Plan exits** — Know your exit before entering
