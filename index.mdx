## What is LiquidLogos?

LiquidLogos is a next-generation perpetual DEX frontend built for professional traders. We bring institutional-grade trading tools to decentralized finance with a clean, intuitive interface.

Our platform is designed with traders in mind — whether you're opening your first position or executing complex strategies, LiquidLogos provides:

- **Professional Trading Terminal** — Advanced charting, order types, and position management
- **Market Discovery** — Deep liquidity across multiple asset classes
- **Portfolio Management** — Real-time P&L tracking and position oversight
- **Risk Analytics** — Monitor exposure, liquidation levels, and margin ratios
- **Liquidity Provision** — Earn yield by depositing into the vault
