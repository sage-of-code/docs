## Analytics Overview

The Analytics page provides market insights and trading data to help you make informed decisions.

## Available Sections

### Market Sentiment

Analyze trader sentiment across markets:

- **Long/Short Ratio** — Percentage of traders going long vs short
- **Whale Movements** — Large trades from major traders
- **Open Interest** — Total notional value of open positions
- **Volume Trends** — Trading activity over time

**Use Cases:**
- Identify potential reversals when sentiment is extreme
- Follow smart money into positions
- Understand market structure

### Snapshots

Time-stamped market snapshots showing:

- **Market prices** — Historical prices at specific times
- **Volume** — Trading volume during period
- **Open Interest** — OI at time of snapshot
- **Top trades** — Largest trades during period

**Use Cases:**
- Understand market structure at specific times
- Identify key price levels
- Track major flows

### Performance Metrics

Track platform metrics:

- **Total Trading Volume** — All-time and period volume
- **Active Traders** — Number of active traders
- **Top Markets** — Most traded assets
- **New Markets** — Recently launched perpetuals

### Your Stats

Personal analytics dashboard:

- **Total Trades** — Number of trades executed
- **Win Rate** — % of profitable trades
- **Avg. Win/Loss** — Average profit/loss per trade
- **Best/Worst Trade** — Largest win and loss
- **Total P&L** — Cumulative profits and losses

## Using Analytics for Trading

### Sentiment Analysis

When sentiment is extreme (>80% long or >80% short):
- Potential for reversal
- Watch for entry opportunities
- Set wider stops to avoid whipsaws

### Volume Analysis

High volume often indicates:
- Strong conviction
- Potential trend continuation
- Good execution opportunity

Low volume:
- Reduced liquidity
- Potential slippage
- Wait for more volume

### Open Interest Analysis

Rising OI with price up:
- Strong uptrend
- New capital entering
- Potential to continue

Falling OI with price down:
- Trend exhaustion
- Liquidations occurring
- Potential reversal

## Market Heatmap

Visual representation of market performance:

- **Size** — Larger blocks = more volume
- **Color** — Green (up), Red (down)
- **Saturation** — Intensity of movement

**Use Cases:**
- Quickly identify strong/weak markets
- Find correlation opportunities
- Spot divergences

## Advanced Metrics

### Liquidation Levels

Heat map showing concentration of liquidation levels:

- **Red zones** — Many stop-losses clustered
- **Blue zones** — Few liquidations
- **Potential impact** — Where liquidations may cascade

### Funding Rates

Historical and current funding rates:

- **Current rate** — APY or BPS
- **Historical chart** — Funding rate trends
- **Impact** — Effect on your holding cost

### Fee Analysis

Fee structure broken down by:

- **Market** — Fee per market
- **Your tier** — Your personalized fees
- **Savings** — Potential fee reduction

## Tips for Effective Analytics Usage

1. **Combine multiple signals** — Don't rely on single metric
2. **Use historical context** — Compare to past events
3. **Monitor during events** — Watch feeds during major news
4. **Track your performance** — Use personal stats to improve
5. **Adjust parameters** — Test different alert thresholds

## Common Use Cases

### Identifying Trends
1. Check sentiment (should align with trend)
2. Monitor OI (should be rising)
3. Watch volume (should be high)
4. Confirm with technicals (charts)

### Finding Entry Signals
1. Look for extreme sentiment readings
2. Monitor volume confirmation
3. Check liquidation clusters
4. Wait for chart confirmation

### Risk Management
1. Monitor liquidation levels
2. Track your leverage vs sentiment
3. Set alerts for key levels
4. Review funding rates
