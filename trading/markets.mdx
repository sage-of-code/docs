## Market Discovery

The Markets page allows you to browse all available perpetual contracts on LiquidLogos.

### Available Assets

LiquidLogos offers perpetual contracts across multiple asset classes:

#### Crypto
- Bitcoin (BTC)
- Ethereum (ETH)
- Solana (SOL)
- And many more altcoins

#### Traditional Assets
- US stocks (AAPL, MSFT, TSLA, etc.)
- Indices (S&P 500, Nasdaq, etc.)
- Commodities (Gold, Oil, etc.)

#### Forex
- EUR/USD, GBP/USD, and other major pairs

### Viewing Market Details

Click on any market to see:

- **Current Price** — Latest traded price
- **24h Change** — Price movement over last 24 hours
- **24h Volume** — Total volume traded
- **Open Interest** — Total notional value of all positions
- **Funding Rate** — Current perpetual swap funding
- **Fee Schedule** — Maker and taker fees

### Sorting and Filtering

Use the toolbar to customize your view:

- **Sort by**: Price, Volume, Change %, Open Interest
- **Filter by**: Asset Class, Price Range, Volume Range
- **Search**: Find markets by symbol or name

### Adding to Watchlist

Click the **★** icon on any market to add it to your watchlist. Access your watchlist from the Trade Terminal.

## Market Statistics

Each market displays real-time statistics:

| Metric | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Last Price** | Most recent trade price |
| **24h High/Low** | Highest and lowest prices today |
| **24h Volume** | Total volume in the last 24 hours |
| **Open Interest** | Total notional value of open positions |
| **Funding Rate** | Current perpetual swap funding (APY) |
| **Avg. Spread** | Average bid-ask spread |

## Understanding Liquidity

Markets are ordered by liquidity to show you which have:

- Tightest spreads
- Fastest execution
- Least slippage

**Higher liquidity = Better trading experience**

Start with highly liquid markets while learning.

## Trading Hours

Most crypto markets trade 24/7. Traditional asset markets follow their exchange hours:

- **US Stocks** — 9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET
- **Forex** — 5:00 PM Sun - 5:00 PM Fri ET
- **Crypto** — 24/7/365

Liquidity is highest during overlapping market hours.

## Funding Rates

Perpetual contracts use funding rates to keep market prices close to spot prices.

- **Positive funding** — Longs pay shorts
- **Negative funding** — Shorts pay longs
- **Your funding** — Paid/received depending on your position

High funding rates can significantly impact your returns on long-term positions.

## Fee Schedule

Fees vary by market and vary based on your VIP level:

| Fee Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Opening Fee** | Charged when you open a position |
| **Closing Fee** | Charged when you close a position |
| **Holding Fee** | Charged based on position age |
| **Liquidation Fee** | Charged if your position is liquidated |

View the exact fees for each market in the market details panel.

## Best Practices

1. **Start with top 5 markets** — These have the most liquidity
2. **Watch trading volume** — Higher volume = better price discovery
3. **Check funding rates** — Avoid shorts when funding is very negative
4. **Monitor spread** — Tighter spreads mean less slippage
5. **Time your entries** — Trade during peak liquidity hours
