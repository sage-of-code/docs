## About the Vault

The Vault is the liquidity backbone of LiquidLogos. Liquidity providers (LPs) deposit capital to:

- **Earn fees** from trading activity
- **Earn interest** from borrowing costs
- **Support the protocol** by maintaining solvency

## Vault Structure

The Vault has two pools:

### Buffer Pool

Used for position margin. When traders deposit collateral, it flows into the Buffer Pool.

**Function:**
- Provides margin to traders
- Earns trading fees
- Exposed to moderate risk

### Insurance Pool

Used for liquidation coverage. Protects the protocol when traders are liquidated.

**Function:**
- Covers liquidation losses
- Earns higher yields
- Higher risk, higher reward

## Providing Liquidity

### Depositing into the Vault

To become an LP and earn yield:

1. Go to the **Vault** page
2. Click **Deposit**
3. Select asset (USDC, USDT, etc.)
4. Choose pool (Buffer or Insurance)
5. Enter amount
6. Approve token transfer (first time)
7. Confirm deposit
8. Wait for confirmation

Your LP tokens represent your share of the pool.

### Withdrawal Process

To withdraw your liquidity:

1. Click **Withdraw**
2. Enter the amount (or select "Max")
3. Review the withdrawal details
4. Confirm withdrawal
5. Wait for blockchain confirmation
6. Assets appear in your wallet

Withdrawals may have cooldown periods during high volatility.

## Earning Yields

### Fee Distribution

LPs earn a share of all protocol fees:

- **Opening fees** — 50% to LPs
- **Closing fees** — 50% to LPs
- **Funding payments** — Adjustments distributed
- **Liquidation fees** — 50% to LPs

Your share is proportional to your vault deposit.

### APY (Annual Percentage Yield)

The Vault displays the current APY for each pool. Yield varies based on trading volume and market conditions — check the Vault page for the latest rates.

### Yield Accrual

Yields accrue continuously as trading fees are distributed. Your share grows proportionally with protocol activity.

## Pool Economics

### Buffer Pool

**Inflows:**
- Trader collateral deposits
- Interest payments
- Fee distributions

**Outflows:**
- Trader withdrawals
- Emergency liquidation coverage

**Risk Level:** Moderate

### Insurance Pool

**Inflows:**
- LP deposits
- Fee distributions
- Protocol insurance contributions

**Outflows:**
- Liquidation losses (rare)
- LP withdrawals

**Risk Level:** Higher (but rare losses)

## Risk Considerations

### Buffer Pool Risks

- **Liquidity risk** — Withdrawals may be delayed if many traders exit
- **Moderate loss risk** — Unlikely but possible during extreme volatility
- **Market risk** — Indirect exposure to trading markets

### Insurance Pool Risks

- **Liquidation losses** — Could lose money if liquidations exceed insurance
- **Concentrated risk** — Loss concentrated on worst days
- **Protocol risk** — If protocol fails, insurance pool could face losses

### Risk Mitigation

LiquidLogos implements:

- **Multi-layer insurance** — Multiple insurance pools
- **Diversification** — Multiple markets reduce concentration
- **Gradual scaling** — LP limits prevent overexposure
- **Governance controls** — Community can adjust parameters

## LP Best Practices

1. **Start small** — Test with small deposit first
2. **Diversify** — Split between Buffer and Insurance
3. **Monitor APY** — Yields change with market conditions
4. **Compound regularly** — Reinvest earnings for compounding
5. **Long-term view** — LPs benefit from sustained trading activity

## Emergency Scenarios

### Bank Run Prevention

If many LPs withdraw simultaneously:

- LiquidLogos implements withdrawal cooldowns
- Gradual withdrawal queuing may apply
- Emergency protocols protect remaining LPs

### Extreme Market Events

If liquidations exceed insurance:

- Protocol has multiple fallback mechanisms
- Governance may activate emergency procedures
- LPs may see temporary withdrawal restrictions

<Tip>
  LiquidLogos is built with multiple safeguards. Catastrophic loss is unlikely but possible.
</Tip>
