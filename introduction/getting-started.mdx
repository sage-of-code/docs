## Prerequisites

To trade on LiquidLogos, you need:

1. **A supported crypto wallet** (MetaMask, Rabby, WalletConnect, Coinbase Wallet)
2. **Collateral on the supported network** (USDC, USDT, or equivalent)
3. **Gas fees** for transactions

## Step 1: Connect Your Wallet

1. Navigate to [liquidlogos.app](https://liquidlogos.app)
2. Click the **Connect Wallet** button in the top right corner
3. Select your preferred wallet provider
4. Review and approve the connection request in your wallet
5. Sign the message to verify wallet ownership (this does not spend funds)

## Step 2: Deposit Collateral

1. Go to the **Portfolio** section
2. Click **Deposit Collateral**
3. Select the asset you wish to deposit (e.g., USDC)
4. Enter the amount
5. Approve the transaction in your wallet
6. Wait for the transaction to confirm

<Note>
  Your collateral is held in the protocol's smart contracts. Only you can withdraw your funds.
</Note>

## Step 3: Open Your First Position

1. Navigate to the **Trade** terminal
2. Click on a market in the **Markets** tab or search by name
3. Choose **Long** (increase price) or **Short** (decrease price)
4. Enter your collateral amount
5. Choose your leverage (1x to 100x)
6. Review the entry price, liquidation price, and fees
7. Click **Open Position**
8. Sign the transaction in your wallet

![Markets](/images/markets.png)

## Understanding the Trade Terminal

The trade terminal has several sections:

- **Watchlist** — Markets you're monitoring
- **Chart** — Price action and technical analysis
- **Order Book** — Buy and sell orders
- **Recent Trades** — Recent transaction history
- **Order Ticket** — Open positions and new order form

<Tip>
  You can use keyboard shortcuts in the Order Ticket (`B`, `S`, `L`, `M`, `A`, `P`, `1-6`, `Enter`) to switch order settings and submit faster. See [Trade Terminal](/trading/trade-interface#keyboard-shortcuts) for the full shortcut table.
</Tip>

## Managing Your Position

Once your position is open:

1. Go to **Positions** to see all open trades
2. View your P&L (profit/loss) in real time
3. Monitor your margin ratio
4. Set take-profit or stop-loss levels if desired
5. Close the position at any time by clicking **Close**

## Monitoring Your Portfolio

Visit the **Portfolio** page to:

- View total equity and margin utilization
- Track collateral balance
- See historical deposits and withdrawals
- Monitor overall account health

## Tips for Success

- **Start small** — Test with small positions to learn the interface
- **Watch liquidation** — Always monitor your liquidation price
- **Use stops** — Set stop-loss orders to limit downside risk
- **Check fees** — Understand the fee structure for each market
- **Study charts** — Use the charting tools to analyze technicals

## Common Questions

**What's the minimum position size?**
Minimum depends on the market. Check the specific market details.

**Can I transfer positions?**
Positions are represented as NFTs and can be transferred to other wallets.

**What happens if I get liquidated?**
Your position is closed at the liquidation price, and your collateral is distributed to liquidity providers and vault participants.

**How do fees work?**
Fees depend on position size, leverage, and holding time. See the specific market for details.
